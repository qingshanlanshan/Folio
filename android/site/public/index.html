Folio

Record meetings and lectures, organised by project, with live transcripts, speakers told apart, and summaries that know the project.

Mac

Everything runs on your Mac: transcription, speakers, and summaries with a local model, or Claude with your own API key.

Download for Mac
Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 14.4 or newer
  • Open the downloaded file and drag Folio into Applications.
  • The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the developer. Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. This is needed only once.
  • On first start, Folio installs its speech engine (about 1.5 GB). Speech models download when first used.
  • Updates: Folio checks for new versions and offers them in the app (Settings → Updates).
  • Optional: keep the Mac and your phone in sync through your own Cloudflare R2 storage (Settings → Sync), encrypted so only your devices can read it.

Android

Speech recognition runs on the phone; summaries, fixes and questions use Claude with your own API key.

Download for Android
Android 10 or newer · 64-bit phones
  • Allow your browser to install apps when Android asks, then open the downloaded file.
  • On first start, Folio downloads its speech models (about 780 MB) — use Wi-Fi.
  • Updates: Folio checks this page and offers new versions in the app.
